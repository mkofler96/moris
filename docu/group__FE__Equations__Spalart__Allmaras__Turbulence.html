Negative Spalart-Allmaras Model

The original model only admits non-negative solutions given non-negative boundary and initial conditions. However, this property is not always obtained numerically. Some configurations on coarse grids and transient states yield a negative turbulence solution. To handle these situations, a continuation model in the realm of negative turbulence solution is used.
