The geometry interpolator class is a specific interpolator that handles the space field and the time field separately,

The geometry interpolator is provided with an integration rule that defines,

  • the geometry type (LINE, QUAD, HEX, ...)
  • the space interpolation type (LAGRANGE, BEZIER, ...)
  • the space interpolation order (CONSTANT, LINEAR, QUADRATIC, CUBIC, ...)
  • the time interpolation type (LAGRANGE, BEZIER, ...)
  • the time interpolation order (LINEAR)

and with a parametric point where the interpolation is carried out.

The geometry interpolator has the ability to provide, at a given parametric point ,

  • the evaluation of the space/time shape functions
  • the evaluation of the first derivatives of the space/time shape functions wrt the parametric coordinates
  • the evaluation of the second derivatives of the space/time shape functions wrt the parametric coordinates
  • the evaluation of the Jacobian mapping
  • the interpolated value of the space/time field , given the discretized values and .
