It is assumed that the residuals are functions of abstract degrees of freedom (adofs). The adofs are defined by a type, such as temperature or displacement in x-direction, and a vector of time step indices.

The time solver can operate on a monolytic or staggered system in time. A staggerd approach triggers another monolytic or staggerd approach. In such a case the the time solvers are stacked in a tree like structure.
